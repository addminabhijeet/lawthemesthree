Frequently Asked Questions

What are the course duration and schedule options?

Our courses are self-paced with flexible learning schedules. You can access materials anytime and complete assignments on your own timeline. Most courses can be completed in 4-12 weeks depending on your pace.

Do you offer certification?

Yes, upon successful completion of any course, you'll receive a digital certificate recognizing your achievement.

What is the refund policy?

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied with the course content or quality.

How can I interact with instructors?

You can reach out via email, live chat, or book a one-on-one consultation with our mentors for personalized guidance.

Are there any prerequisites?

Most courses are open to anyone interested in legal education. Some advanced courses may require prior knowledge of basics.
